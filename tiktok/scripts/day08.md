**Pillar:** Comment restore · **Length:** ~60s · **Photo:** the best
submission from day 7. Pick on STORY, not damage. If submissions are thin,
use a planned backup but keep the series framing ("while your photos come
in, here's one from a viewer on another platform" — only if literally true;
otherwise run a normal restore and launch the series tomorrow).
