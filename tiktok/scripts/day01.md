# Day 1 — Launch: the oldest family photo you have

**Pillar:** Story restore · **Length:** ~65s · **Photo:** your own — the
oldest/most meaningful family photo you can find tonight. If none, use a
public-domain family portrait and adapt the story honestly ("this family is
strangers to me, but someone out there is missing this photo").

## Voiceover (word for word)

> **HOOK:** "This is the only photo that exists of them together."
>
> "It's been sitting in a drawer for fifty years. Every year it fades a
> little more — the faces first, then everything else. Most families have
> one of these. A photo that's almost gone.
>
> So I'm going to bring it back. Watch.
>
> *(process plays — one line at the halfway beat:)*
> "Watch the faces come back first. That's always the part that gets me."
>
> *(reveal — pause 2 seconds, then:)*
> "There they are.
>
> I'm going to do this every single day. Drop a photo in the comments —
> tomorrow it might be yours."

## Shot list / edit

1. 0.0–0.4s — flash the finished AFTER, hard cut
2. 0.4–3s — the before, slow zoom, hook line
3. 3–15s — before, closer crops on the damage while the story plays
4. 15–45s — sped-up screen capture: upload → processing → result forming
5. 45–55s — REVEAL: slow push-in on the after, minimal talk
6. 55–65s — side-by-side before/after, CTA line, hold the frame
