# Day 7 — Series launch: "one from the comments, every day"

**Pillar:** Story restore → series announcement · **Length:** ~55s ·
**Photo:** one more of your own — but the video's real job is launching the
comment-restore series that powers the rest of the account.

## Voiceover

> **HOOK:** "I'm restoring one photo from the comments every day. Day one
> is mine — after this, they're yours."
>
> "This week I brought back six photos. A face from 1943. A photo everyone
> called unfixable.
>
> *(quick 3-beat montage of the week's befores→afters, 1s each)*
>
> "But my drawer is running out. Yours isn't.
>
> *(today's restore plays, fast — 10s:)*
>
> "So here's the deal. Drop a photo below. Tell me who's in it, one line.
> Every single day I pick one, restore it, and tag you.
>
> Day one of your photos starts tomorrow."

## Shot list / edit

1. AFTER flash → hook to camera... no camera — hook over the montage
