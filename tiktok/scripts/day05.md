# Day 5 — "Watch her face come back"

**Pillar:** Story restore · **Length:** ~60s · **Photo:** one where a face is
almost lost — blur, fade, damage across the eyes. The single-face restore is
the highest-emotion format in the niche; save it for day 5 when the account
has a pulse.

## Voiceover

> **HOOK:** "Watch her face come back."
>
> "This is somebody's mother. And for thirty years, this blur is all they've
> had of her.
>
> No editing tricks. No repainting. Just recovery.
>
> *(process — say nothing for 5 full seconds, then:)*
> "There. The eyes are always the moment.
>
> *(reveal, long pause:)*
> "If there's a face like this in your family — drop the photo below. Every
> day, I pick one."

## Shot list / edit

1. AFTER flash (face only, tight crop) → cut to the blurred before
2. Very slow zoom toward the face for the whole first section
3. Screen capture cropped TO THE FACE — this video stays tight the whole way
4. Reveal: hold the restored face full-frame for 3 seconds, no text on it
5. Side-by-side of just the face, then full photo, CTA
