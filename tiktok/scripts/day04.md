# Day 4 — The faded 70s photo

**Pillar:** Story restore · **Length:** ~60s · **Photo:** a color photo from
the 60s–80s with the classic orange/beige fade. Nearly every family album
has ten of these.

## Voiceover

> **HOOK:** "The 70s weren't beige. Your photos just gave up."
>
> "This is what fifty years does to color film. The reds go first, then the
> blues, until everyone looks like they lived inside a filter.
>
> But the color isn't gone — it's just buried.
>
> *(process — halfway beat:)*
> "This isn't guessing new colors. It's pulling the original ones back out."
>
> *(reveal:)*
> "That's the actual shirt. That's the actual sky.
>
> Check your parents' albums tonight — then drop one here. I do this daily."

## Shot list / edit

1. AFTER flash → cut to the faded before
2. Push in on the most faded region during the hook
3. Screen capture; use a before/after slider drag as the process visual
4. Reveal with a saturation "pop" cut, not a fade
5. Side-by-side + CTA hold
