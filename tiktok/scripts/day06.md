# Day 6 — "He was 19 here" (WW2 portrait)

**Pillar:** History · **Length:** ~30s · **Photo:** public-domain WW2-era
service portrait (Library and Archives Canada, Library of Congress, Wikimedia
Commons). Pick a young face — the hook depends on it.

## Voiceover

> **HOOK:** "He was 19 here. This is what he actually looked like."
>
> "Black and white makes the past feel far away. Like it happened to other
> kinds of people.
>
> *(colorization sweeps:)*
> "It didn't. He's just a kid. Same age as the guy who delivered your food
> today.
>
> One photo back every day. Follow, or better — send me one."

## Shot list / edit

1. Colorized AFTER flash → b&w original
2. Slow push on the face during the hook
3. Color wipe reveal, then close crops: eyes, uniform detail, skin tone
4. End: side-by-side, dates on screen ("1943 → today"), CTA
