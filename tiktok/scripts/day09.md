# Day 9 — Torn in half, rejoined

**Pillar:** Story restore · **Length:** ~65s · **Photo:** a photo torn in
two (or with a piece missing). Source: thrift-store photo boxes, family, or
a submission. The tear should cross something that matters.

## Voiceover

> **HOOK:** "Someone tore this in half forty years ago. Watch."
>
> "Photos don't get torn by accident. An argument. A divorce. A grudge
> somebody took to the grave.
>
> Whatever it was — time's up.
>
> *(process — halfway beat:)*
> "Seam first. Then the missing pieces get rebuilt from what's left."
>
> *(reveal, pause:)*
> "Back together. Whether they liked it or not.
>
> Got a torn one? You know where to put it."

## Shot list / edit

1. AFTER flash → the two torn halves held apart, then pushed together
   (if physical: film this 5s on your phone — it's the best before-visual
   this account will ever get; if digital: animate the halves in CapCut)
2. Macro on the torn edge during the hook
3. Screen capture: alignment → seam removal → inpainted gap
4. Reveal + push-in
5. Side-by-side + CTA

## Post

- **Caption:** `Forty years apart. Ten seconds back together. 🧩 Drop yours below.`
- **Hashtags:** #photorestoration #beforeandafter #torn #oldphotos
- **AIGC label: ON**
- **Honesty note:** rebuilt regions are AI inpainting — say "rebuilt", not
  "recovered", for missing pieces. The label covers you legally; the wording
  keeps the audience's trust.
