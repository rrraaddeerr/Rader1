# Monetization — Canada-viable revenue plan

## The constraint, stated once

Operating from Canada means **no Creator Rewards Program** (requires US/UK/
DE/JP/KR/FR/MX/BR residency) and **no TikTok Shop seller/affiliate program**
(no Canadian marketplace). Do not VPN around this — payouts require matching
ID and tax residency, and mismatches get accounts banned at exactly the
moment they become valuable.

Everything below works from Canada from day one.

## Revenue ladder (in the order to activate)

### 1. Affiliate — the restoration tool itself (day 1)

You demo a tool every single day; the viewer's next question is always
"what app is that?" Answer it with an affiliate link, in the bio and in a
pinned comment on every video.

Programs to apply to (pick the tool you actually use — see PRODUCTION_SOP):

- **MyHeritage affiliate** (photo tools + genealogy) — pays per trial/sub,
  genealogy conversions are high-value
- **Remini** — via their partner/affiliate program where available
- **VanceAI / Topaz Labs** — direct affiliate programs, ~15–30% on sales
- **ElevenLabs** (if you use it for voiceover) — recurring commission

Expected: negligible until ~5k followers, then $100–500/mo per 100k monthly
views in this niche. Recurring-commission tools compound.

### 2. Paid restorations — the service (week 2)

"Send me your photo — $15, back within 48h." This is the sleeper income:
comment submitters are pre-qualified buyers, and the marginal cost of a
restore is minutes.

- Start with a Ko-fi / Google Form + e-transfer or Stripe Payment Link.
  No website needed.
- Price ladder: $15 single photo → $40 for 3 → $100 "family pack" of 10
  with a short reveal video of each.
- Turn every paid job (with permission) into a video. The service feeds
  the content; the content sells the service.

Expected: at even modest scale (50k monthly views), 10–30 orders/mo is
realistic in this niche = $150–900/mo, growing with the account.

### 3. Cross-posting (week 1, zero extra work)

Post every video to Instagram Reels + YouTube Shorts the same day (strip the
TikTok watermark — export clean from CapCut). YouTube Shorts DOES pay
Canadian creators via the Partner Program (1k subs + 10M Shorts views/90d or
4k watch hours), so the same content has a real payout path there even
though TikTok's doesn't reach Canada. Treat TikTok as the growth engine and
YouTube as the eventual salary.

### 4. Brand deals (at ~10k followers)

Genealogy services, photo-book printers (Chatbooks, Mixbook), scanning
services, and the AI tools themselves sponsor this niche. Rate of thumb:
$100 per 10k followers per sponsored video, more with strong engagement.
List the account on TikTok Creator Marketplace alternatives that accept
Canadians (e.g. Collabstr) once past 10k.

### 5. Digital product (month 3+, optional)

"Restore your own family photos" mini-guide ($19–29) or preset/workflow
pack, sold via Gumroad/Ko-fi. Only build this after the audience is asking
questions that a guide would answer.

## What NOT to spend money on

- Faceless-account courses, "AI TikTok automation" SaaS, engagement pods,
  follower buys (instant algorithmic sandbox).
- Ads to grow the account. Organic or nothing at this stage.
- More than one restoration tool subscription after week 1's tool test.

## Bookkeeping note

Affiliate + service income is self-employment income in Canada. Track from
dollar one (a spreadsheet is fine). If it becomes real, GST/HST registration
kicks in at $30k/12mo — a good problem, note it and move on.
