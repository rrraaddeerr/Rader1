## The one-paragraph plan (alternate lane: restoration)

Post one before/after video per day for 30 days in ONE sub-niche.
Primary lane: **AI photo restoration** — old, damaged, or black-and-white
photos restored/colorized on screen. The transformation is real (the AI
actually did it), so it's honest AI use, it's labeled and compliant with
TikTok's 2026 AI rules, and every video is producible in under 30 minutes
with no filming. Money comes from affiliate links to the restoration tools
(recurring 15–30% commissions), a paid "send me your photo" service, and
brand deals — NOT from TikTok's Creator Rewards, which isn't available in
Canada anyway.

## Day-one checklist

1. Read STRATEGY.md (10 min).
2. Do everything in ACCOUNT_SETUP.md (30 min).
3. Sign up for one restoration tool + its affiliate program (MONETIZATION.md).
4. Produce video 1 from `scripts/day01.md` using PRODUCTION_SOP.md.
5. Post between 6–9pm PT. Log it in tracker.csv.
6. Repeat daily. Do not evaluate anything before video 20.

## Rules of the road (do not break these)

- **One niche.** No mixing restoration content with other before/afters
  until 10k followers.
- **Label AI content** with TikTok's built-in AIGC label on every video.
  Detection is automated (C2PA watermarks); unlabeled = throttled or removed.
- **Never fabricate a "before."** The account's whole value is that the
  transformation is real.
- **No reposted/compilation content.** Unoriginal content is ineligible for
  the For You feed entirely.
- **Judge hooks, not videos.** A flop with 70% 3-second retention is a good
  video with a bad topic. A flop with 20% retention is a bad hook.
