# Strategy — Before & After, Faceless, AI-Assisted

## The theme

**Before and after.** Every video on this account shows a real transformation
with a tease → process → reveal structure. The theme is the format; the
sub-niche below is the subject.

## Primary lane: AI photo restoration

Old, damaged, faded, or black-and-white photos restored and colorized with AI,
shown on screen as a before/after.

Why this lane wins for an AI-gen faceless account in 2026:

1. **It's the honest AI lane.** TikTok's 2026 rules bar *fully AI-generated*
   video from Creator Rewards and throttle unlabeled synthetic content. But
   restoration content isn't faking anything — the AI transformation IS the
   content. Label it AIGC and you're fully compliant.
2. **Zero filming.** Screen recording + voiceover. Every video producible in
   under 30 minutes from a desk. Daily cadence is actually sustainable.
3. **Emotional payoff.** "She'd never seen her mom in color" outperforms
   almost every other before/after on shares and comments. Shares and saves
   are the heaviest-weighted signals in 2026's algorithm.
4. **Built-in engagement engine.** "Drop a photo in the comments and I'll
   restore one every day" turns the comment section into a content pipeline
   and guarantees replies/duets.
5. **The monetization matches Canada.** Creator Rewards requires residence in
   the US/UK/DE/JP/KR/FR/MX/BR and TikTok Shop has no Canadian seller
   program — so a Canadian account earns via affiliate links and services.
   AI restoration tools pay recurring 15–30% affiliate commissions, and
   "send me your photo, $15/restore" is a service you can sell from day one.

## Content pillars (all before/after, all restoration)

- **Restores** (core, ~4/week): one photo, tease → process → reveal.
- **Comment restores** (~2/week): "Day N of restoring a photo from the
  comments." Screenshot the comment, restore, tag the person.
- **History colorized** (~1/week): public-domain historical photos
  (Library of Congress, city archives) brought to color. Broad reach plays.

## The format contract (every video)

1. **0.0–0.5s** — flash the AFTER (or a split-frame) before anything else.
   Never open on the before alone.
2. **0.5–3s** — cut to the before + spoken hook.
3. **3s–80%** — the process: sped-up screen capture, 2–3 beats of voiceover
   telling the photo's story (the story carries watch time, not the tech).
4. **80%** — full reveal, slow push-in on the after.
5. **Last 2–3s** — before/after side-by-side lingering shot (screenshot bait).
6. **CTA** — one of: "drop a photo in the comments" / "link in bio for the
   tool" / "follow for tomorrow's restore."

Length: 60–90 seconds for story restores (keeps the door open for US
monetization programs later, which require 1min+), 15–25s for quick history
colorizations.

## Backup lane: thrift flip before/after

If restoration stalls by video 30 (see kill criteria below), pivot to thrift
flips — $5 find → cleaned/tailored/styled reveal. Hands-only filming, same
format contract, monetized with Amazon/LTK affiliate links for the products
used. This lane also feeds the futureoutfit project directly. Do NOT run both
lanes on one account.

## Kill / double-down criteria (evaluate at video 20 and 30)

- **Double down** if: any video >10k views, OR median 3-second retention
  >55%, OR follower count >500.
- **Fix hooks first** if: retention <40% — the topic is fine, the opens are
  weak. Rewrite hooks on the 5 best-retained videos' pattern.
- **Pivot lanes** only if: 30 videos posted, median views <300, zero videos
  broke 5k, and comment engagement is flat. Anything better than that is
  normal slow-start noise.

## What this account never does

- Fake or staged "befores" — one exposed fake kills the account's premise.
- Restoring identifiable private people's photos WITHOUT permission — comment
  submissions are permission; scraping someone's family photos is not.
- Reposts, compilations, other creators' restores — FYF-ineligible.
- Unlabeled AI content — C2PA watermark detection makes this a guaranteed
  throttle, escalating to account penalties.
- Mixing niches before 10k followers.
