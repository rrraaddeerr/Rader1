# Production SOP — the daily 30-minute workflow

## Tool stack

| Job | Tool | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Restoration/colorization | Remini, MyHeritage (In Color), VanceAI, Topaz Photo AI — pick ONE as your daily driver after testing | ~$10–40/mo |
| Screen recording | macOS: Cmd+Shift+5 / QuickTime; phone: built-in recorder | free |
| Editing + captions | CapCut (auto-captions, beat sync, speed ramps) | free |
| Voiceover | Your own voice (best), or ElevenLabs reading YOUR script | free–$5/mo |
| Source photos | Comments (once flowing); Library of Congress, Wikimedia Commons, city archives (public domain); your own family albums | free |
| Thumbnails / profile art | Canva | free |

Test 3 restoration tools on the same damaged photo in week 1; keep whichever
gives the most dramatic honest result. Your affiliate link should be for the
tool you actually use.

## The daily loop (target: 30 min)

1. **Pick the photo (5 min).** Priority: comment submissions > public-domain
   with a strong story > own archive. Strong story beats strong damage.
2. **Restore it (5 min).** Screen-record the ENTIRE process, including the
   upload and the loading moment — the "processing…" beat is the suspense.
3. **Write the script (5 min).** Use the template below. Hook first, write
   it last-word-perfect; the rest can be loose.
4. **Record voiceover (3 min).** One take, conversational, slightly slower
   than feels natural.
5. **Edit in CapCut (10 min).**
   - Frame 1: the AFTER, 0.4s flash (or split-screen), then hard cut to before
   - Speed-ramp the process footage to fit the voiceover
   - Auto-captions ON, positioned upper-middle (thumb zone is dead space)
   - Reveal at ~80% with a slow push-in
   - Final 2–3s: side-by-side before/after, hold
   - Trending-but-quiet sound under the voiceover at ~10% volume
6. **Post (2 min).**
   - Caption: one line of story + question ("Whose photo should I do next?")
   - 3–5 hashtags: #photorestoration #beforeandafter #restoration + one
     topical (#ww2 #1950s #vintagephoto)
   - **Toggle the AI-generated content label ON** — every time, no exceptions
   - Post 6–9pm PT (or test 7–9am PT as the alt slot in week 2)
7. **Log it (1 min).** Add the row to tracker.csv.
8. **First hour after posting:** reply to every comment. Velocity of early
   engagement is a ranking input, and comments are tomorrow's content.

## Script template

```
HOOK (≤10 words, spoken over the before):
  "<line that names the stakes or the person>"

STORY (2–3 beats, ~15s):
  Who is in the photo / what happened to it / why it matters.

PROCESS BEAT (~5s):
  One line about what the AI is recovering ("watch the eyes come back").

REVEAL (silent or one line):
  Let it breathe. "There she is." beats a paragraph.

CTA (1 line):
  "Drop a photo in the comments — I restore one every day."
```

## Compliance checklist (every video)

- [ ] AIGC label toggled on
- [ ] Photo is: a comment submission, public domain, or your own
- [ ] No fabricated damage / staged before
- [ ] No music louder than the voiceover (muted-original-sound videos die)
- [ ] Over 60s if it's a story restore

## Batching (optional, week 3+)

Sunday: pick 7 photos, run 7 restorations, record all screen captures.
Daily: script + voice + edit + post one. Cuts daily time to ~20 min.
