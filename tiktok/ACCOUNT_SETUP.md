# Account Setup

Do all of this before posting video 1. Budget ~30 minutes.

## Handle

Pick one (check availability, shortest available wins):

- `restored.daily`
- `the.restore.desk`
- `bring.them.back` (emotional angle)
- `colorized.co`
- `restoreroom` / `the.restore.room`
- `afterlight.restores`

Rules: no numbers, no underscores if avoidable, must be sayable out loud.
Reserve the same handle on Instagram + YouTube the same day (you'll
cross-post Reels/Shorts for free reach later — same videos, zero extra work).

## Display name

`Name | Photo Restorations` — e.g. **The Restore Room | Photo Restorations**.
The words "Photo Restoration" in the display name is search SEO; TikTok
search is a major discovery surface in 2026.

## Bio (pick one, keep under 80 chars)

- `I bring old photos back to life 🖼️ → drop yours in the comments`
- `Restoring one photo from the comments every day • Day 1`
- `Your grandparents deserve to be seen in color`

Link in bio: start with a Linktree/Beacons page containing (1) the
restoration tool affiliate link, (2) "Get your photo restored — $15" order
form (Google Form or Ko-fi to start), (3) futureoutfit/rent.co later, once
traffic exists. See MONETIZATION.md.

## Profile image

No face. Options: a striking half-restored photo split down the middle
(strongest — it IS the theme), or a simple wordmark. Generate the split-image
in Canva in 10 minutes.

## Settings checklist

- [ ] Personal account (NOT business — business accounts lose access to the
      full sound library and some features; switch later only if needed)
- [ ] Region/att: leave as-is; do not VPN-spoof a US location to chase
      Creator Rewards — payout requires matching ID/tax residency and
      mismatches get accounts banned
- [ ] Turn ON the AIGC self-label setting and label EVERY restore video
      as AI-generated content
- [ ] Enable analytics (Creator tools → Analytics) from day one
- [ ] Set up comment filters: block nothing initially — comments are the
      content pipeline
- [ ] Pinned video slot: after video 5, pin your best performer

## Warm-up (days 1–2 before first post)

New accounts that post instantly with zero activity get sandboxed more often.
For two days: scroll the FYP 15 min/day, search "photo restoration" and
watch/like the top creators' videos, follow 10–15 accounts in the niche.
This also seeds your FYP so you can study what's working.

## Competitor study (1 hour, once)

Find the top 5 accounts in the niche (search: "photo restoration",
"restored photo", "colorized"). For each, note in tracker.csv's notes tab:
their hook pattern, video length, sound choice, and their 3 most-viewed
videos. Copy structure, never content.
