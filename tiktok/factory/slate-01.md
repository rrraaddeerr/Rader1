# Slate 01 — ten Stuff videos, seeded pre-dump

Every script follows the video contract in CONCEPT.md: after-flash → real
object + hook → three why-beats → 2030 reveal → side-by-side + stamp.
All AI-gen visuals labeled AIGC. Claims correspond to rows in
CLAIMS_LEDGER.csv (claim_id in each header). 35–60s each.

Format of the AI-gen prompts: written for an image/video generator
(Runway/Kling/Veo-class). Generate the "2030" shots; film the "today" shots
on your phone in 10 seconds — a real object grounding each video makes the
render land harder and keeps the account from feeling like pure synth.

---

## 1. The Wallet — `S01-wallet`

**Hook:** "This thing in your pocket is on its last four years."

**Beats:**
1. "Every EU country has to hand its citizens a government digital ID
   wallet — the deadline was December 2026. That's not a startup promise,
   that's law."
2. "Licenses, diplomas, prescriptions — verified from the phone, sharing
   only the one fact needed. Age without address. Name without number."
3. "By 2030 your wallet is one dead card and a sentimental photo. Ask
   your grandpa about his checkbook."

**Today shot:** your actual wallet, opened, contents fanned on a table.
**2030 AI-gen prompt:** *macro product shot, slim card holder containing a
single transparent polymer card and a folded paper photo, resting on a
bedside table next to smart glasses, soft morning light, shallow depth of
field, photorealistic, muted 2030 minimalism* → second shot: *POV holding a
phone at a bar, screen shows only "OVER 19 ✓" in green, bartender nodding,
no other personal data visible.*

**Caption:** `Your wallet in 2030: one dead card and a photo. Bookmark this. 🧾`
**Stamp:** CALLED IT — JUL 2026

## 2. The Phone Glance — `S01-glasses`

**Hook:** "In 2030, checking your phone like this will look like this."
*(pull out flip phone)*

**Beats:**
1. "Smart glasses grew 167 percent in a year. Meta's doubling production
   because they can't keep Ray-Bans on shelves."
2. "The glance moves to the lens: directions, translations, who's calling —
   without breaking stride. The phone stays in the pocket like the laptop
   stayed in the bag."
3. "Twenty million pairs this year. Fifty million a year by decade's end.
   The people staring down at screens become the anachronism."

**Today shot:** street footage (faceless, over-shoulder) of hands scrolling.
**2030 AI-gen prompt:** *street-level shot, person seen from behind walking
through a crosswalk wearing ordinary-looking glasses, subtle heads-up
directions floating in their lens visible as a faint reflection, everyone
around them heads-up, phones nowhere visible, golden hour, photorealistic.*

**Caption:** `The phone isn't dying. The GLANCE is. 👓 See you in 2030.`

## 3. The Clothing Tag — `S01-dpp`

**Hook:** "Every tag in your closet is about to get a brain."

**Beats:**
1. "The EU is rolling out Digital Product Passports — new clothes will
   carry a scannable record: what it's made of, where, how to repair it,
   what it's worth used."
2. "Which means secondhand gets receipts. That thrifted jacket can prove
   what it is. Fakes get harder. Good clothes hold value like cars with
   service records."
3. "By 2030 you'll scan a tag at the thrift store and see its whole life.
   Buying blind will feel ancient."

**Today shot:** close-up flipping through tags in a closet / thrift rack.
**2030 AI-gen prompt:** *macro shot, fingers holding a woven garment tag
with a subtle iridescent QR weave, phone hovering above it showing a clean
timeline UI — mill, factory, first sale, resale, repair — thrift store
racks blurred in background, photorealistic, warm tungsten light.*

**Caption:** `Thrifting in 2030 comes with receipts. Literally. 🏷️`

## 4. The Cable Drawer — `S01-charger`

2. "Magnetic Qi2 pads are doing to cables what wifi did to ethernet at

3. "By 2030 it's one cable and one pad for everything you own. This
   drawer becomes the junk museum your kids laugh at."

**Today shot:** the universal tangled-cable drawer, opened dramatically.
**2030 AI-gen prompt:** *overhead shot of a minimal desk: a single braided
USB-C cable coiled neatly and one slim magnetic charging pad with a phone,
earbuds case and glasses stacked on it, single drawer open below containing
only a passport and a pen, photorealistic, clean morning light.*

**Caption:** `RIP the junk drawer, 2007–2029. 🔌 Bookmark it.`

## 7. The Paper Receipt — `S01-receipt`

**Hook:** "This 40-centimeter pharmacy receipt is an endangered species."

**Beats:**
1. "France already banned automatic paper receipts. Thermal paper's coated
   in chemicals you're not supposed to touch, and nobody wants the litter."
2. "E-receipts land in the wallet app, itemized and searchable — returns
   without the shoebox of paper."
3. "By 2030, paper is opt-in at every major retailer. The CVS-length
   receipt becomes a meme your feed explains to teenagers."

**Today shot:** unrolling an absurdly long real receipt down a hallway.
**2030 AI-gen prompt:** *shot of a checkout counter, cashier gesturing at a
small screen reading "receipt sent ✓", customer's phone showing a clean
itemized list auto-filed under 'Groceries', no paper anywhere,
photorealistic, fluorescent retail light made beautiful.*

**Caption:** `Your grandkids will not believe the CVS receipt. 🧾 Saving this one AS a receipt.`

## 10. The Price Tag — `S01-shelf`

**Beats:**

2. "Prices update from an office, not a ladder — which enables dynamic

**2030 AI-gen prompt:** *grocery aisle, every shelf edge a crisp e-ink

photorealistic, bright clean retail light.*

**Caption:** `Groceries are getting surge pricing. Called it. 🛒 See you at the receipt.`
