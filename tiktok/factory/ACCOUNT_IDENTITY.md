## Profile image

The stamp itself: "CALLED IT" in the brand font inside a rubber-stamp
circle, off-axis 8°, ink-red on off-white. No face, no robot imagery —
rubber stamp, not chrome future. Make once in Canva, never change it.

## Voice rules

- Confident, specific, a little smug — never breathless. "That's not a
  startup promise, that's law" is the register.
- Numbers and dates out loud in every video; they're the credibility.
- Never say "insane", "mind-blowing", or "the future is here."
- The account is FROM 2030 looking back, so the past tense is available
  for style: "cash retired quietly. you kept a twenty in the glovebox."

## Pinned content strategy

1. Pin the best-performing Stuff video (rotate monthly).
2. Pin the first receipt video FOREVER once one lands — it's the proof
   the whole account runs on.
3. Pinned comment on every video: the claim, restated in one line, with
   "screenshot this" — screenshots are distributed receipts.
