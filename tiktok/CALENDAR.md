# 30-Day Content Calendar

One post per day. Days 1–10 have full scripts in [scripts/](scripts/).
Days 11–30 have concept + hook; write the script the same day using the
template in PRODUCTION_SOP.md. Pillar key: **R** = story restore,
**C** = comment restore, **H** = history colorized.

Videos 1–7 use your own/public-domain photos to seed the account. From
day 8, comment submissions should start replacing them — always prefer a
real submission over a planned concept.

## Weekly rhythm notes

- **Day 7 and 20 are structural:** day 7 launches the comment-restore series
  (the engagement engine), day 20 is the checkpoint — run the kill/double-down
  criteria from STRATEGY.md before posting day 21.
- **Day 14 and 30 recaps** are cheap to make and historically over-perform —
  they're pure reveal density.
- **Day 28 (tool comparison)** is the affiliate video. Pin it, put the link
  in the pinned comment, and expect it to convert for months.
- If a comment submission has a great story, it ALWAYS bumps the planned
  concept. The calendar is the fallback, not the boss.
